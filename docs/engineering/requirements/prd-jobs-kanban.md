## Summary

Route production work through the shop's work centres and show it on a live board.

## Known requirements (to be expanded)

- Typical route: design, proof, print, laminate, cut or route, fabricate, install. Stages are configuration.
- The kanban board is a view of jobs, not a separate record.
- Must work well on a tablet or wall screen on the shop floor.
- Installation jobs need a site address, contact, date and crew.
- Vehicle wraps may need vehicle details (make, model, registration).
