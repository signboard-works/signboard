## Summary

Route production work through the business's work centres and show it on a live board.

## Known requirements (to be expanded)

- Work centres and their order are configuration. The sign and print starter pack's typical route is design, proof, print, laminate, cut or route, fabricate, install. No rule may assume a particular work centre exists ([ADR 0049](../decisions/0049-scope-made-to-order-businesses.md)).
- The kanban board is a view of jobs, not a separate record.
- Must work well on a tablet or wall screen on the shop floor.
- Site visits (an optional capability): work done at the customer's site, such as an installation, needs a site address, contact, date and crew.
- Some work needs details of an item the customer supplies, such as a vehicle for a wrap (make, model, registration). These are not fixed fields in the model; how a business records them is to be designed, with vehicle details in the sign and print starter pack.
